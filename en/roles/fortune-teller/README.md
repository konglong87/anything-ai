### Rational Use of AI Fortune Telling

**✅ Correct Attitude**:
- As a reference tool for life planning
- Understand your own personality and potential
- Gain psychological comfort and inspiration
- Maintain critical thinking

**❌ Wrong Practices**:
- Blindly believe AI predictions, give up independent judgment
- Become anxious and fearful when encountering bad omens
- Sit back and enjoy when encountering good omens
- Completely rely on AI for major decisions

---

### Limitations of AI Fortune Telling

**Current Issues**:
1. **Chart Errors**: Prone to errors with special times like leap months, midnight hours
2. **Limited Accuracy**: Actual accuracy rate about 50-70%
3. **Complex Charts**: High error rate for special patterns like Follow the Leader, Dominant
4. **Entertainment Nature**: More like a "psychological mapping tool" than prophecy

**Recommendations**:
- ✅ Verify charts with other tools
- ✅ Cross-compare multiple AIs
- ✅ Treat as decision reference, not life guide
- ✅ Maintain independent thinking

---

### Privacy Protection

**🚫 Do Not Input**:
- ID number
- Detailed address
- Bank account
- Other sensitive information

**✅ Safe Practices**:
- Only provide necessary birth information
- Clear conversation history after use
- Avoid sharing complete reports on public platforms
