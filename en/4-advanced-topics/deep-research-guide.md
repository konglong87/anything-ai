# Deep Research Feature Guide

> Generate Complete Research Reports in Minutes

---

## 🤔 What is Deep Research?

**Deep Research** is the latest capability of AI tools that can automatically generate complete industry research reports, travel guides, policy interpretations, and other complex task reports within minutes.

### Core Features:
- **Automated** - Multi-round search, thinking while searching
- **Structured** - Generates complete reports with clear logic
- **Multi-format** - Supports report documents, visual webpages, podcasts
- **High Efficiency** - Completes hours of work in minutes

## 📋 Use Cases

### ✅ Scenarios Suitable for Deep Research

#### 1. Long-distance Travel Planning
```
Prompt: "Help me plan a 7-day Japan travel guide, including Tokyo, Kyoto, Osaka, budget 20,000 yuan"
```

**Doubao will automatically**:
1. Search for latest attraction information
2. Query transportation and accommodation
3. Understand local weather and specialties
4. Generate complete itinerary planning

## 🎯 Usage Techniques

### 1. Provide Detailed Background
```
❌ Bad: "Research AI market"
✅ Good: "Research 2026 China AI large model market, focus on application scenarios and business models"
```

### 2. Clarify Output Requirements
```
❌ Bad: "Give me a guide"
✅ Good: "Generate a 7-day Japan travel guide, including itinerary, budget, precautions"
```

### 3. Specify Focus Dimensions
```
❌ Bad: "Analyze competitors"
✅ Good: "Analyze competitors from three dimensions: product features, price, user reputation"
```

| Dimension | Deep Research | Regular Q&A |
|------|---------|---------|
| **Search Rounds** | Multi-round iteration | Single search |
| **Information Integration** | Systematic integration | Simple listing |
| **Output Format** | Structured report | Short answer |
| **Applicable Scenarios** | Complex tasks | Simple questions |
| **Processing Time** | A few minutes | A few seconds |

## ⚠️ Important Notes

### 1. Data Timeliness
- AI information has cutoff dates
- Latest developments suggest cross-verification
- Important data check original sources

### 2. Information Accuracy
- AI can make mistakes, need to verify
- Multi-source cross-verification
- Critical information verify official materials

### 3. Copyright Issues
- Generated content attention to copyright
- Commercial use requires caution
- Cite information sources
